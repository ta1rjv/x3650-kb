Placement rules (DOCUMENTED):

  • Hot-swap models only: "RAID is supported in hot-swap models only" (S-002 specs note 1).
  • ServeRAID controllers sit on the SAS riser card in a PCIe x8 mechanical slot with x4 electrical; "the controllers run at x4 bandwidth" (S-002 specs note 2).
  • With up to 8 bays the RAID controller uses a dedicated RAID slot and does not consume a regular PCIe slot; with 16 bays the regular PCIe slots drop to three (S-001 after Table 8; S-001 Table 7 text for 59Y3825 and 69Y5034).
  • Server lists "up to 5 PCIe 2.0 x8 slots (4 usable, 1 for RAID)" (sources/SOURCES.md key facts, from S-001).

5. Drive bays, backplanes, SAS riser and cabling #

5.1 Bay configurations (DOCUMENTED) #

S-001 "Internal storage": four 2.5" SATA simple-swap bays (CTO only), four hot-swap, eight hot-swap, sixteen hot-swap, or eight hot-swap plus internal tape. S-002 (Table 1 "Features and specifications", depending on the model): eight 2.5" SAS hot-swap bays with the option to add eight more, or four 2.5" simple-swap SATA/SSD bays; "What your server offers": up to eight or sixteen 2.5" hot-swap drives depending on model and options. Bay IDs 0-7 (and 0-15 with the 8-pack option) are printed on the front above each bay (S-002 figures 1-3). All bays are 2.5" SFF: there are no 3.5" hard-disk bays in this machine type; the 3.5" items in S-002 are tape/RDX bezels, and 3.5" drives appear in S-001 only as external EXP enclosure options.

Standard models: A2x ships with four bays, all other models with eight (S-001 "Internal drive configurations").
