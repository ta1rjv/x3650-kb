Key facts and conflicts (for the topic documents) #

  • TIPS0805: 2U; up to 2x Xeon 5600; Intel 5520 chipset; 18 DIMM sockets (9 per CPU); up to 16x 2.5-inch SAS/SATA; up to 5 PCIe 2.0 x8 slots (4 usable, 1 for RAID); 2x GbE + optional 2x GbE card; PSU 460 W, 675 W, 675 W HE AC; IMM with IPMI 2.0, SNMP v3, optional Virtual Media Key. Model 7945-22x = 1x E5606, 1x 4 GB, M1015, 1x 460 W (closest to 7945KEG).
  • ZG11-0012: dual Broadcom 5709 GbE; IMM shares one onboard port; IPMI 2.0 implemented with "OSA firmware"; 6 hot-swap fans (3 pairs); altimeter sensor; internal USB for embedded ESXi 4.1; model suffix "G" = EMEA.
  • Conflicts: chipset 5520 (TIPS0805) vs "5600" (ZG11-0012 p.4); max memory 288 GB vs 192 GB; PSU 81Y6558 "465W" vs 460 W elsewhere. Resolve with S-002/S-004.
  • Parts (S-006): system board 81Y6625; 675 W HE PSU 39Y7218; fan 49Y5361; backplanes 43V7070, 69Y0650, 94Y6670; risers 69Y4324 (PCIe x8), 69Y4326 (PCI-X). The owner's board FRU reads 94Y7614 / 69Y5082 (captures/2026-10-19_ipmi_inband.txt) - relation to 81Y6625 is UNKNOWN.
